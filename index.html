Method & interpretation

About the data

Four OECD datasets

The dashboard combines cleaned OECD data for life expectancy, health expenditure, preventive healthcare and selected risk factors.

14-country core group

The comparison group is the intersection of countries available across all four processed datasets, identified during the project's cross-dataset audit.

Different reporting patterns

Time coverage varies by dataset. Prevention views show valid observations for the selected year, while risk factors use each country's latest available observation.

Interpret with care

The dashboard supports exploration and comparison; it does not establish causal relationships between spending, prevention, risks and health outcomes.

Source: OECD health datasets supplied and cleaned for the COS30045 project. Values shown are based on the processed CSV files included with this submission.
